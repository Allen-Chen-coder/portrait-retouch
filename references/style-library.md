## 1. 日常清透 Natural Clean（默认安全牌 / default）
- **氛围 / Mood**：像没修过，只是皮肤状态好、光线好。/ Looks untouched — just good skin and good light.
- **适配 / Suits**：一切原图，尤其用户要求"自然"时。/ Everything, especially when the user says "natural".
- **风格词 / Style words**：natural clean look, subtle brightening, true-to-life colors, minimal retouching
- **注意 / Caution**：几乎不加风格层，全靠修图动作本身。/ Almost no style layer; the retouch actions do the work.

## 2. 冷白皮 · 氧气感 Cool Fair
- **氛围 / Mood**：干净、冷调、通透，韩剧女主皮。/ Clean, cool, translucent — K-drama lead skin.
- **适配 / Suits**：室内暖光偏黄、肤色暗沉的照片；白皮用户慎用（会显惨白）。/ Warm yellow indoor light, dull skin. Caution for already-pale users (can turn ghostly).
- **风格词 / Style words**：cool-toned fair skin, airy and translucent complexion, soft cool color grading, high-key lighting feel
- **注意 / Caution**：只降暖不提亮度过多；保留皮肤纹理，否则会塑料。/ Reduce warmth without over-brightening; keep skin texture or it turns plastic.

## 3. 暖调奶油肌 Creamy Warm
- **氛围 / Mood**：温柔、亲和、奶茶色系，适合秋冬感穿搭。/ Gentle, friendly, milk-tea palette; autumn/winter outfits.
- **适配 / Suits**：冷白皮过曝照、想要亲和力的场景。/ Overexposed cold-toned shots;亲和力-driven contexts.
- **风格词 / Style words**：warm creamy skin tone, soft caramel color grading, gentle warm light, cozy mood
- **注意 / Caution**：暖不等于黄，肤色仍要干净。/ Warm ≠ yellow; keep skin clean.

## 4. 日系小清新 Japanese Fresh
- **氛围 / Mood**：透明感、空气感、微微过曝的午后阳光。/ Transparency, airiness, slightly overexposed afternoon sun.
- **适配 / Suits**：户外、校园、JK/休闲穿搭、浅色背景。/ Outdoors, campus, casual/JK outfits, light backgrounds.
- **风格词 / Style words**：Japanese fresh style, airy transparency, soft overexposed highlights, low-contrast pastel tones, gentle film feel
- **注意 / Caution**：阴影要浅、对比要低，但五官不能跟着被冲淡。/ Shallow shadows, low contrast — but features must not wash out.

## 5. 韩系画报 Korean Editorial
- **氛围 / Mood**：精致、妆感完整、打光均匀的杂志内页。/ Polished, full makeup, evenly lit magazine page.
- **适配 / Suits**：想"出片"的社交照、写真级需求。/ Social-ready photos; editorial-level requests.
- **风格词 / Style words**：Korean editorial photoshoot look, polished makeup, soft studio-like lighting, clean pastel background tones
- **注意 / Caution**：画报感最容易出 AI 味，皮肤纹理和发丝细节必须强调保留。/ Editorial looks most easily trigger AI vibes — emphasize skin texture and hair detail.

## 6. 港风复古 HK Retro
- **氛围 / Mood**：90 年代港片，浓颜、暖金、对比强、故事感。/ 90s Hong Kong cinema: rich features, warm gold, strong contrast, storytelling.
- **适配 / Suits**：黑发、红唇适配度极高；夜景街拍尤其好。/ Great with black hair and red lips; superb for night street shots.
- **风格词 / Style words**：90s Hong Kong cinematic style, rich warm golden tones, strong contrast, deep shadows, nostalgic film look, subtle halation on highlights
- **注意 / Caution**：对比拉高的同时保住暗部肤色，不要变成"黄脸"。/ Keep shadow skin tones while raising contrast — no "yellow face".

## 7. 胶片感 · CCD 直出 Film / CCD
- **氛围 / Mood**：颗粒、轻微色偏、闪光灯直打的怀旧数码感。/ Grain, slight color cast, nostalgic early-digital snapshot feel.
- **适配 / Suits**：朋友聚会、日常记录，遮画质瑕疵一把好手。/ Parties, daily records; great at hiding quality flaws.
- **风格词 / Style words**：CCD digicam aesthetic, direct flash look, visible film grain, slight color cast, casual snapshot vibe, authentic early-2000s digital photo
- **注意 / Caution**：颗粒和偏色是"做旧"，不是画质差；人物主体仍要清晰。/ Grain and cast are "aging", not bad quality; the subject stays sharp.

## 8. 法式慵懒 French Effortless
- **氛围 / Mood**：不经意的精致，柔光、低饱和、米色/燕麦色系。/ Effortless polish, soft light, low saturation, beige/oat palette.
- **适配 / Suits**：浅色穿搭、卷发、午后户外。/ Light outfits, curls, afternoon outdoors.
- **风格词 / Style words**：French effortless chic, soft diffused light, muted neutral palette, slightly desaturated warm tones, candid elegance
- **注意 / Caution**：低饱和不等于灰，唇色和眼神要留住重点。/ Desaturated ≠ gray; keep lips and eyes as focal points.

## 9. ins 博主风 · 高级感灰调 Minimalist Gray
- **氛围 / Mood**：性冷淡、clean fit、低饱和灰米色，适合穿搭照。/ Minimalist, clean-fit, desaturated gray-beige; for outfit shots.
- **适配 / Suits**：全身/半身照、穿搭展示、城市背景。/ Full/half body, outfit display, urban backdrops.
- **风格词 / Style words**：minimalist Instagram aesthetic, desaturated grey-beige tones, clean composition, subtle matte finish
- **注意 / Caution**：灰调下肤色容易脏，需要单独写 keep skin tone healthy and natural。/ Gray grading dirties skin easily — add "keep skin tone healthy and natural".

## 10. 黑白情绪肖像 B&W Mood Portrait
- **氛围 / Mood**：去色后的情绪和结构感，经典肖像。/ Emotion and structure without color; classic portraiture.
- **适配 / Suits**：侧脸、轮廓光、表情有内容的照片。/ Profiles, rim light, expressive faces.
- **风格词 / Style words**：black and white fine-art portrait, rich tonal gradation, dramatic but natural light, timeless editorial feel
- **注意 / Caution**：黑白最能暴露磨皮塑料感，皮肤纹理要求提到最高。/ B&W exposes plastic smoothing the most — maximize skin-texture requirements.

## 11. 形象照 · 职场专业 Professional Headshot
- **氛围 / Mood**：干净、可靠、精神；浅色背景、均匀布光。/ Clean, trustworthy, alert; light background, even lighting.
- **适配 / Suits**：求职照、工牌、领英头像、讲师介绍。/ Job hunting, badge, LinkedIn, speaker bio.
- **风格词 / Style words**：professional headshot style, even soft lighting, clean neutral background, crisp and trustworthy look, subtle sharpening
- **注意 / Caution**：换背景时边缘融合和发丝细节是重灾区；表情必须与一致原图（见 anti-ai.md §5）。/ Background swaps: edge blending and hair detail are danger zones; keep the exact expression (anti-ai.md §5).

## 12. 夜景霓虹 · 氛围感 Neon Night
- **氛围 / Mood**：城市夜色 + 霓虹反光，冷蓝与暖橙对冲。/ Urban night + neon reflections, teal-orange contrast.
- **适配 / Suits**：已有夜景灯光的原图，切勿把白天 P 成夜晚（违和重灾区）。/ Night shots with existing lights; never turn day into night (classic failure zone).
- **风格词 / Style words**：neon night city vibe, teal and orange color contrast, ambient city glow on skin, cinematic night mood
- **注意 / Caution**：光源方向必须与原图夜景灯光一致。/ Light direction must match the original night lights.

## 13. 油画感 · 古典肖像 Classical Oil Portrait
- **氛围 / Mood**：伦勃朗光、暗背景、古典油画质感。/ Rembrandt light, dark background, classical oil-painting feel.
- **适配 / Suits**：正装、深色穿搭、侧光照片。/ Formal wear, dark outfits, side-lit photos.
- **风格词 / Style words**：classical oil-painting portrait style, Rembrandt lighting, dark muted background, painterly yet photorealistic skin, old-master color palette
- **注意 / Caution**："画意"靠光影和色调，不靠把皮肤画糊；面部仍是照片级清晰。/ Painterliness comes from light and color, not from smearing skin — the face stays photo-sharp.

## 14. 美式复古 · Yearbook American Yearbook
- **氛围 / Mood**：美式证件照/毕业年鉴风，蓝灰色幕布、均匀闪光。/ American yearbook style: blue-gray backdrop, even flash.
- **适配 / Suits**：想要"美式复古证件照"梗、酷感表情。/ The retro yearbook trend; cool expressions.
- **风格词 / Style words**：American yearbook photo style, blue-grey backdrop, direct on-camera flash, retro 90s school portrait
- **注意 / Caution**：蓝幕换背景时注意肩线边缘和发丝；保持一点闪光灯的硬光感才像。/ Mind shoulder lines and hair on backdrop swaps; keep some hard flash feel to sell it.

## 15. 柔焦梦幻 · 白月光 Dreamy Soft-Focus
- **氛围 / Mood**：轻微柔焦、朦胧光晕、言情小说封面感。/ Gentle soft focus, hazy glow, romance-novel-cover mood.
- **适配 / Suits**：唯美写真需求、白裙/浅色穿搭。/ Dreamy portraits; white dresses/light outfits.
- **风格词 / Style words**：dreamy soft-focus portrait, gentle glow, diffused highlights, ethereal romantic mood
- **注意 / Caution**：柔焦只加氛围层，眼睛和五官必须锐利清晰，否则一秒 AI 感。/ Soft focus is an atmosphere layer only — eyes and features stay razor sharp or it screams AI.

## 16. 素颜好气色 No-Makeup Healthy（男生/中性向 / men & neutral）
- **氛围 / Mood**：看不出妆感，只是睡得好、气色好、轮廓干净。/ No visible makeup — just well-rested, healthy, clean contours.
- **适配 / Suits**：男性用户或偏好无妆感的用户。/ Men or no-makeup users.
- **风格词 / Style words**：natural no-makeup healthy look, even skin tone, subtle definition to jawline and brows, masculine/fresh and clean, no visible makeup
- **注意 / Caution**：绝对避免让皮肤变"白嫩"，只均匀肤色和去疲劳感；胡须/眉形按用户要求处理。/ Never turn skin "pale and tender" — even tone and remove tiredness only; handle beard/brows per request.
